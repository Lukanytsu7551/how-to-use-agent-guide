# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

How to Use Agent serves readers who want to choose, understand, and use AI agents in real work. The audience includes first-time Codex or WorkBuddy users, learners studying agent foundations, developers bringing agents into local projects, creators and knowledge workers using agents for writing and research workflows, and team leads who need repeatable rules, examples, governance practices, and safety boundaries.

## Product Purpose

How to Use Agent is a practical tutorial and reference site with three connected learning entrances: Codex Guide, WorkBuddy Guide, and the book-style Agent Guide. It helps readers choose the right product for a concrete task, complete a first task, understand agent foundations and advanced configuration, reuse proven examples, and turn successful workflows into team-ready practice.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, practical, and systems-oriented.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing pages, decorative card walls, vague AI productivity claims, over-minimal pages that hide the actual tutorial depth, and noisy community pages that make the site feel less credible.

## Design Principles

- Show the learning path before asking readers to choose.
- Keep dense information scannable through hierarchy, rhythm, and concrete labels.
- Treat tutorials as the product: surface high-value chapters and examples, not abstract promises.
- Keep the Agent Guide book structure visible: foundations first, then tools, systems, governance, and runtime engineering.
- Preserve trust with restrained color, readable typography, and direct copy.
- Make contribution and governance paths visible without overwhelming the learning flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Support reduced motion for animated homepage elements. Keep links meaningful out of context, make scrolling galleries readable when motion is disabled, and ensure mobile readers can access the same content without hover or precise pointer input.
